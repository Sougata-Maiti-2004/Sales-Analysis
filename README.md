# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and delivery efficiency. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful visual insights, helping businesses make informed and data-driven decisions.

## Major Insights

### Occasion-wise Revenue Analysis
The dashboard analyzes revenue generated across various occasions such as:

- Anniversary
- Birthday
- Diwali
- Holi
- Raksha Bandhan
- Valentine's Day

**Key Finding:**  
Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generate the highest revenue, indicating strong seasonal demand and customer engagement during these events.

### Category-wise Performance
Revenue is analyzed across multiple product categories, including:

- Colors
- Soft Toys
- Sweets
- Cakes
- Mugs
- Plants

**Key Finding:**  
Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets are the top-performing categories, while Mugs and Plants contribute comparatively lower revenue.

### Product Analysis
The dashboard identifies top-selling products and their revenue contribution.

**Key Benefit:**  
Helps businesses understand customer preferences and optimize inventory management.

### Monthly Sales Trend
Monthly revenue trends are visualized to identify seasonal fluctuations and peak sales periods.

**Key Finding:**  
Revenue peaks during festive and special-event months, reflecting seasonal purchasing behavior.

### City-wise Order Analysis
Order distribution across different cities is analyzed to identify high-demand markets.

**Key Benefit:**  
Supports targeted marketing strategies and efficient logistics planning.

### Revenue by Order Time
Revenue is analyzed based on order placement time throughout the day.

**Key Benefit:**  
Helps identify peak purchasing hours for scheduling promotions and marketing campaigns.
